The result is a system where money flows faster than it can be checked.

1. PROTECTING THE SHIFA FUND AGAINST PRIVATE SECTOR LEAKAGE

The government subsidises up to 60% of Bahraini patient treatments in private hospitals. Employers carry mandatory health packages for expatriate workers. This creates a complex, multi-payer environment that is a textbook breeding ground for upcoding — billing for a more expensive procedure than was delivered — and duplicate claims filed across multiple payers for the same episode of care.

FRAUDIQ operates as an automated, real-time auditing firewall. Every private hospital claim is cross-referenced against standard clinical pathways before funds are released — whether from the SHIFA fund, an employer insurance pool, or a commercial insurer. The flag happens before payment, not after.

For every $1M in claims processed, our detection engine typically surfaces $80,000–$240,000 in anomalous billing.

―――――――――――――――――――――――――――――

2. CLOSING THE PHARMACEUTICAL PROCUREMENT GAP

The Ministry of Health has identified medicine overspend as a primary budget deficit driver. FRAUDIQ's pharmaceutical anomaly detection layer identifies:
— Phantom prescriptions (prescriptions issued without a corresponding clinical encounter)
— Doctor-shopping patterns (patients obtaining the same controlled substance from multiple providers)
— Clinic over-ordering and hoarding relative to registered patient volumes
— Formulary substitution fraud (dispensing a lower-cost drug, billing for a higher-cost one)

These patterns are invisible to manual auditors. They are not invisible to FRAUDIQ.

―――――――――――――――――――――――――――――

3. EXPAT UTILISATION INTEGRITY

With a large expatriate workforce dependent on employer-sponsored coverage, Bahrain faces structural pressure on private clinic billing volumes. FRAUDIQ's cross-employer, cross-provider analytics detect coordinated billing anomalies across the expat population — flagging clusters of inflated claims from specific clinics or employer pools before they become systemic losses.
