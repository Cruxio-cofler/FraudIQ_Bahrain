03 — Detection Architecture

Six Engines. One Firewall.

Drawn directly from FraudIQ's live US payer platform — the same six-stage detection pipeline, configured for Bahrain's Sehati fund, NHRA pathways, and multi-payer environment.

STAGE 01 · TEAL
Ingestion & Triage
Every incoming claim is OCR-parsed, fingerprinted, de-duplicated, and assigned a FIQ risk score before any human reviews it.
Document hash + line-item fingerprint catches re-submitted claims under new IDs
Completeness gate: missing consents, blank clinical fields, absent severity scores
Blended risk score: provider history × claim-vs-peer ratio × document completeness
🇧🇭 Catches: duplicate Sehati + employer cross-filings
STAGE 02 · BLUE
Member & Benefit Vault
Claim is matched to the member's coverage, full claims history, prior authorisations, and EHR — including cross-payer coordination of benefits.
Eligibility, plan validity, sub-limits and exclusions vs diagnosis + place of service
Prior-auth vs final claim diff — LOS creep and scope expansion flagged
EHR vault + COB: parallel claims with other payers cross-linked
🇧🇭 Catches: expat identity reuse across multiple employer pools
STAGE 03 · PURPLE
Document Forensics
Before trusting any clinical data, FraudIQ authenticates the paper: NPI/facility IDs, signatures, scan metadata, and physiologically impossible values.
Signature & credentials graph — NPI vs procedure billed vs facility registration
Impossible-value detector: vitals that cannot physiologically coexist
Identity contradictions: blood group mismatches, device metadata conflicts
🇧🇭 Catches: fabricated NHRA-format clinical forms
STAGE 04 · RED · CORE
Clinical Cross-Match
The detection core. Bill reconciled against every clinical artefact — physician notes, nursing notes, labs, vitals, drug charts, operative notes — scored for medical necessity.
Bill vs notes vs labs vs drugs vs vitals — every mismatch scored
Medical necessity scoring against NHRA-configured clinical pathways
Upcoding detection: procedure billed vs documented clinical justification
🇧🇭 Catches: upcoding to inflate Sehati reimbursement
STAGE 05 · AMBER
Coding & Pricing Audit
Every code and price is audited against reference pricing and code-pair rules — unbundling, NCCI edits, and pharmaceutical formulary ceiling checks.
Upcoding, unbundling, and code-pair conflict detection
Drug pricing: claimed cost vs NHRA-registered formulary ceiling
Phantom prescriptions: prescription issued without corresponding clinical encounter
🇧🇭 Catches: pharma over-billing, formulary substitution fraud
STAGE 06 · SLATE
Adjudication & Network Intelligence
Final verdict plus persistent network mining — provider patterns, coordinated fraud rings, and cross-employer expat billing clusters surface over time.
Provider Intelligence Score: cumulative anomaly history across all claims
Network pattern mining: coordinated billing clusters across provider + patient + employer
SIU referral, disallowance, and full explainability audit trail for NHRA
🇧🇭 Catches: coordinated expat billing rings across clinics
⚡
All six stages run before payment authorisation. Traditional audit firms operate after payment — recovering <20 cents per dollar, months later. FraudIQ runs the full six-stage pipeline in real time, stopping the loss before the fund transfers — not recovering it after.
